# Article Catalog — cubicle.id

Scope: 90 planned Indonesian-language knowledge and project-support assets for office, toilet, and electrical cubicle systems. The catalog supports the commercial supplier/installer route without turning neutral education into sales copy.

Domain boundaries: `cubicle.id` may independently cover subjects also discussed by other owned domains. Cannibalization controls apply only within this domain. `/` owns the current offer, proof, contact, and quote conversion. No city/province swap pages are permitted. Electrical work is specialist-only; accessibility, fire, moisture, material, installation, cleaning, and procurement claims must pass the gates in `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md`.

Catalog statistics: 15 parent topics; 90 briefs; exactly 6 briefs per topic. Wave A contains 12 bounded first-cluster assets, Wave B 34 foundation/selection assets, Wave C 33 lifecycle/diagnostic assets, and Wave D 11 specialist/context assets.

Priority legend: `P0` = first-cluster or critical safety/trust foundation; `P1` = next decision/lifecycle coverage; `P2` = deeper operational/context coverage; `P3` = evidence-dependent specialist expansion.
